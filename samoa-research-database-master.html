Six-dimension geography

The dashboard is built against Samoa's six independent geography dimensions — never assumed interchangeable:

  1. Statistical Region (4 SBS census regions)
  2. Political/Census District (~43 SBS districts)
  3. Village (~340 SBS Village Directory entries)
  4. Specific Island (independent field)
  5. Traditional Itūmālō (11 constitutional districts)
  6. Electoral Constituency (time-versioned; 2019 Act = 51 territorial + 2 individual-voter)
