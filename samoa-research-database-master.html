Samoa Scholar Database

A curated, evidence-based record of scholarly research by and about Samoan and part-Samoan postgraduates and researchers. Every scholar carries six independent geography dimensions on both paternal and maternal sides — Statistical Region, Political/Census District, Village, Specific Island, Traditional Itūmālō, and Electoral Constituency — matching the structure of the SBS statistical spine, the Constitution's Second Schedule, and the Electoral Constituencies Act 2019.
