Pacific Islander Faculty Beach Gathering — Interest & Planning Form

Aloha kākou,

We are exploring the possibility of hosting an informal beach gathering for Pacific Islander faculty from BYU–Hawaiʻi and UH Mānoa. The purpose is simply to connect, relax, build relationships, and create space for Pacific faculty across institutions to meet one another outside of formal university settings.

The gathering will likely be held on a Saturday in late July or early August on Oʻahu. Please complete this short form to help us decide the best date, location, format, and activities.
