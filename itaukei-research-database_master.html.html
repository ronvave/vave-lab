About this database

What's counted

One row per confirmed iTaukei scholar in the roster, with relational sheets for graduate degrees, scholarships and funding, awards and honours, and current or past appointments. A scholar with both a Master's and PhD counts once here but twice in degree-episode totals.

Provenance

Records were assembled from university theses catalogues, gazetted graduation notices, institutional biographies, personal communications, and public genealogical statements. Paternal and maternal province, district and village are recorded only when supported by explicit biographical evidence — never inferred from a surname, thesis location, or generic “from” statement.

What's not counted

Confederacy is a separately curated field and is not automatically derived from province. An Unclassified paternal province means the record has not yet been verified — it is not a claim about the scholar. Awards, funding, or leadership records absent here mean absent from public evidence at time of curation.

Companion database

Publication counts and journal-article records live in the sibling iTaukei Scholarly Research Database. Both are curated by Prof. Ron Vave.
