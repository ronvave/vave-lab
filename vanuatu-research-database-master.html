A3 Inclusion, geography and data coverage

Identity is verified using person-specific evidence, never a name or a Vanuatu research topic. Scholar community affiliations, birth/upbringing, study institutions and research sites are distinct. Paternal and maternal affiliations are kept separate; neither is a required identity proxy.

All panels use approved Master records and stable VAN identifiers. Missing geography remains unclassified. A work or scholar is counted once at each selected place; totals summed across places can exceed the unique total. Degree completion alone does not establish a publication.

Province → Island → Area Council / Locality → Village / Community is used only where recorded relationships exist. The official area-council register parents councils to provinces, not inferred islands. Port Vila, Luganville and Lenakel are municipal jurisdictions, not ordinary area councils.

Geographic registry: Department of Local Authorities provinces and area councils, checked 3 October 2026. Map geometry: geoBoundaries / OpenStreetMap, 2017 boundaries, ODbL 1.0; reference boundaries do not certify present-day council limits.
