Vanuatu Scholar Database

This database highlights the scholarly research and contributions of ni-Vanuatu postgraduates and researchers, in Vanuatu and around the world. It is curated by Assistant Professor Ron Vave at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa as part of a regional scholarly database initiative. This growing resource supports visibility, networking, collaboration and mentorship.

About this database · Inclusion criteria and methodologyA3

What is included

Verified, display-approved journal articles, books, book chapters, Master’s theses, doctoral theses and reports. Other or unknown types, conference items, preprints and unpublished works are excluded from counted outputs but remain in the Master File.

Who is counted

Only verified, display-approved ni-Vanuatu scholars enter these public totals. Neither paternal geography nor a surname establishes identity. Degree episodes, scholars and thesis publications are separate measures.

Geography and multiple attribution

Research sites are separate from paternal and maternal community affiliations, universities and workplaces. Each publication counts once per place; totals across places can overlap. Missing location is not evidence of no research.

Updates and known gaps

The Vanuatu Master File is authoritative. Only allowlisted fields enter the snapshot. Unavailable fields display a dash rather than a false zero. Refresh timing is shown when a snapshot loads.

Province reference boundaries: geoBoundaries / OpenStreetMap, 2017, ODbL 1.0. These boundaries do not certify current area-council limits.
