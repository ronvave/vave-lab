About this database · Inclusion criteria & methodology

What's included

Scholarly publications where Fiji or Fijians are the primary geographic, cultural, or thematic focus, and where at least one of the following applies:

  • Peer-reviewed journal articles, book chapters, and books
  • Master's, PhD, and equivalent higher-degree theses
  • Technical reports and conference papers from recognised institutions
  • Preprints archived on established repositories

Special emphasis on work authored or co-authored by iTaukei (Indigenous Fijian) scholars, regardless of subject discipline.

How tags are assigned

  • Province researched — assigned to the province(s) explicitly named as fieldwork or study sites in the abstract, methods, or first paragraph. Multi-province studies are tagged to each relevant province.
  • Paternal province — applied only to iTaukei first-authors, derived from author biographical statements, personal communication with the author where possible, and public genealogical records.
  • Discipline — a single primary discipline based on the publishing journal, thesis department, or dominant methodological framing. Cross-cutting works are placed under the strongest thematic fit.

Update cadence

The underlying Zotero group library is the source of truth and is updated continuously. This page loads a cached snapshot for fast rendering and checks the live library each visit to flag additions. The snapshot itself is regenerated on a monthly cadence, or sooner when a substantial batch is added.

Known gaps & caveats

  • Pre-1970s materials and colonial-era grey literature are underrepresented.
  • Unpublished traditional-knowledge documentation held by communities is deliberately excluded pending appropriate protocols.
  • Non-English publications from regional universities and Pacific-language journals may be missing where the item is not indexed by major databases.
  • Some paternal-province assignments remain provisional pending author confirmation; entries without a paternal-province tag are treated as unclassified in that view.

Corrections, additions, and suggestions are welcome — email ronvave@hawaii.edu or use the Contribute panel below.
