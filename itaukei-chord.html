Scholar Mobility

Interactive Visualization

Master’s → PhD Institutional Pathways

This chord diagram traces where iTaukei (Indigenous Fijian) scholars completed their Master’s and PhD degrees. Each ribbon links a scholar’s Master’s institution to their PhD institution, coloured by the country of the Master’s degree. Reading from the outside in, the rings show region (UNSD M49), then country, then each numbered university. Self-loops (same institution for both degrees) are retained. It forms part of the iTaukei Research Database.

↓ Download CSV template

Your file stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. CSV and Excel (.xlsx/.xls) files both work; for Excel the first worksheet is used. The scholar file needs the columns m_uni, m_country, p_uni, p_country, and either a scholar column or first + last. Regions are derived automatically from the UNSD reference; you only need the second file to change that mapping.
