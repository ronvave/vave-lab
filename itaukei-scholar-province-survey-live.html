iTaukei scholar province survey

This page collects paternal and maternal village and province information for iTaukei Masters and PhD scholars so the research site can credit scholarly productivity by home province.

The public page allows open contribution and only shows progress counts by scholar. Full submitted details should stay restricted to the admin view.
