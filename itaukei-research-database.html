iTaukei Scholarly Research Database

A curated, publicly-accessible Zotero database of scholarly research on Fiji and Fijians — with a deliberate focus on iTaukei (Indigenous Fijian) authors. Explore by province where the research was conducted, by 1st-author's paternal province (grouped by chiefly confederacy), or by country and university where iTaukei theses were completed.
