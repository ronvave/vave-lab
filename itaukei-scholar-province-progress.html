What this data unlocks

Once we know each scholar's paternal and maternal village and province, the public dashboard can do things it currently cannot:

  • Filter the whole publication database by paternal province — see who from Ba, Rewa, Cakaudrove, and so on has published what.
  • Show the same view by maternal province — a side the literature almost never captures.
  • Group scholars by confederacy (Kubuna, Burebasaga, Tovata) and let communities see their own scholarly footprint.
  • Give every scholar's profile page a proper vanua line, so their work sits in place, not just on a shelf.
