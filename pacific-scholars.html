        E

        Publications

        Every publication linked to a scholar in the Regional Scholar Directory, combined across all countries and listed once even when scholars from more than one country share it. The circle flag shows the country of the linked scholar; hover over it for their name. Like the country dashboards, it counts only the six main publication types: journal articles, book chapters, books, PhD theses, Master's theses and reports. Conference papers and other outputs are left out. Panel A4 (Publications over time) charts this same list by year. Forthcoming and in-press items are marked and grouped at the end.
